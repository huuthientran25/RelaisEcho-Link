Guided camera checkup · orbit to the North

Your last checkup, lined up with today.

A phone or laptop camera coaches you into the same position as your baseline, measures only when the signal is good, and prepares a short packet a remote practitioner can review.

Research prototype built at NASA Space Apps 2026, Montreal. Not a diagnostic device. If something feels wrong, contact a clinician directly.

What it captures

  • Pulse estimate from face video, with a quality score
  • Breathing rate from neck and upper body movement
  • Matched dental photos: front and both sides

What is new here

  • No wearable and no upload: video never leaves this device
  • Compared with your own baseline, not a population average
  • Works offline and builds a short packet a nurse can review

    Brain clearance and sleep

    EDUCATIONAL

    After about six months on the ISS, MRI showed larger fluid spaces around brain blood vessels (perivascular spaces) in astronauts, larger in NASA crew than in cosmonauts.

    Whether sleep speeds up the brain's waste clearance (the glymphatic system) is still debated: a 2024 mouse study found clearance went down during sleep, and the original group disputed it in 2025.

    No glymphatic number is shown, because none can be measured with a camera.

    Alertness check · experimental

    3 minute reaction test

    A counter starts at random moments. Press the big button, or the space bar, the moment you see it. Tired brains miss more and respond slower, often before you feel it.

    • Same device, same posture, same time of day as last time, so results compare fairly.
    • Do not press before the counter appears.
    • Your first full test on this device becomes your reference.

      Experimental checks
      
    
      Experimental checks
      
    
      
      
      Seven short camera checks. Each result is compared only with your own first result of the same check, on the same kind of device. None is validated and none is a diagnosis.
      
      
      The blood pressure and oxygen items are indexes without units. A webcam has no cuff and no infrared light, so it cannot give mmHg or a saturation percentage, and the app never shows one.
      
      
      ! Chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness, a sudden change in vision, or a neck lump that grows quickly needs direct medical care. Do not wait for these checks.

            Community and mission use

This prototype has no such agreement and claims none.
