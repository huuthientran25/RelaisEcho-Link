Guided camera checkup · orbit to the North

Your last checkup, lined up with today.

A phone or laptop camera coaches you into the same position as your baseline, measures only when the signal is good, and prepares a short packet a remote practitioner can review.

Research prototype built at NASA Space Apps 2026, Montreal. Not a diagnostic device. If something feels wrong, contact a clinician directly.

What it captures

  • Pulse estimate from face video, with a quality score
  • Breathing rate from upper torso movement
  • Matched dental photos: front and both sides

What it refuses to do

  • Blood pressure, oxygen or fever from a camera
  • Diagnose tooth decay, SANS or any disease
  • Give a number from a capture that failed its checks
